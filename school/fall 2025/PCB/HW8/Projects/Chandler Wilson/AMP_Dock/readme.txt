Title: AMP Dock
REV: 1.0-A

Description:
The AMP Dock is a power conversion circuit that consists of two 
different buck converters that take battery inputs from a Milwaukee M18 
and a DeWalt XR. 
The system steps the voltage of these batteries down to feed a 12 V bus 
that is used to output 12 V to power a fish finder device or other similar 12 V 
powered device. The 12 V bus is also parallelly connected to a separate buck 
converter that steps down to 5V for USB-A and USB-C outputs for phone charging. 
This device will be contained in a IP67 container that has mounting points for 
the batteries and USB output ports.
