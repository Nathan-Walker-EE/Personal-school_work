This project is simple as it takes in a mono audio source
does a voltage division goes into an op amp and outputs it 
to another mono audio output. there is a 3 pin connector 
for power and GND. 

There are errors in the ERC. This is due to Kicad labeling pins
on schematic symbols. They are expecting certain inputs and outputs

time. Although electrically the circuit is sound and there is not "real"
